Amazon CodeCatalyst enables teams to collaborate on features, tasks, bugs, and any other work involved when building software. CodeCatalyst was announced at re:Invent 2022 and is currently in preview. Introduction: In a prior post in this series, Using Workflows to Build, Test, and Deploy with Amazon CodeCatalyst , I discussed reading The Unicorn Project , by Gene Kim , and how the main character, Maxine, struggles with a complicated software development lifecycle (SLDC) after joining a new team.
